3. Map tiles (BKG TopPlusOpen)

The interactive map on the Map view tab loads base-map tiles (TopPlusOpen) from the Federal Agency for Cartography and Geodesy (Bundesamt für Kartographie und Geodäsie, BKG), Richard-Strauss-Allee 11, 60598 Frankfurt am Main, Germany. When the map is displayed, your IP address and standard browser information are transmitted to BKG servers in Germany to deliver the tiles. No map content is loaded before you open that tab. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in displaying interactive map content). Details: BKG privacy policy.

4. Visitor statistics (GoatCounter)

To see how many people use this site, it counts visits with GoatCounter, a statistics service operated by Martin Tournoij, Ireland. When you open the dashboard, your browser loads a small script from gc.zgo.at and sends one request to eu-ads.goatcounter.com. This transmits the page address, the page you came from, your IP address and standard browser information (browser, operating system, language, screen size). According to its privacy policy, GoatCounter stores only aggregated counts per day or hour, for example by browser or country. Your IP address and the full browser identification are not written to a database; they stay in memory for up to eight hours so that repeat visits are not counted twice. GoatCounter stores nothing in your browser and shares no data with third parties. The data are held on servers of Hetzner Online GmbH in Finland and Germany. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in knowing how much the site is used). You can prevent the count by blocking these two addresses with a content blocker; the dashboard works the same without them. Details: GoatCounter privacy policy.

5. Fonts

This site uses only the fonts already installed on your device (system font stack). No fonts are loaded from Google Fonts or any other third-party service.

6. Cookies and local storage
